## About

I am **Salvatore Sinatra**, a freelance photographer and visual artist based in **Caltagirone, Sicily, Italy**.

My photographic practice spans **fine art, editorial, portrait, black-and-white, urban, architectural, and landscape photography**. I investigate the relationships between **light, form, presence, atmosphere, space, and perception**, with a particular focus on visual coherence and authorial intent.

I am also an **undergraduate student in Psychological Sciences and Techniques (L-24)** at **eCampus University**. My academic interests include cognitive psychology, visual perception, cognitive neuroscience, neuropsychology, and experimental methodology.

Alongside photography, I explore **creative technology, generative AI, digital imaging, visual communication, and independent web publishing**.

## Creative technology & web publishing

I use GitHub to organize and develop independent digital projects that connect **photography, editorial design, archival research, and web publishing**.

### Featured repository

**[music-archives-italia](https://github.com/salvatoresinatra0001-ux/music-archives-italia)**  
An independent repository dedicated to Italian-language music-archive websites and their evolving editorial structures. **In development.**

Related website concepts include dedicated archives for **Assemblage 23**, **The Birthday Massacre**, and **Ville Valo / HIM / Heartagram**. These are independent editorial initiatives, not official artist websites.

## Principles

**Photographic integrity.** Respect for original images, authorship, visual intention, and faithful presentation.

**Editorial clarity.** Precise composition, carefully structured content, and restrained visual systems.

**Independent research.** Source-aware documentation, clear attribution, and distinctions between verified information and interpretation.
